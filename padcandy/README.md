# Candy Garden (wop_padcandy)

An FFA map inspired by the candy room of a famous sweet factory (user's reference stills): an emerald meadow under a
glass factory roof where everything is edible, a chocolate river fed by a chocolate waterfall pouring off a layered
limestone cliff, giant mushrooms, crepe-paper flower trees, a gumball tree, lollipops, candy canes, swirl-striped
jawbreakers, gummy bears, black iron urns heaped with gumballs, an iron pump-house deck with candy-coloured pipes dipping
into the river, and the entrance balcony with its ornate iron railings and grand staircases.

Padman is gummy-bear sized (~200u per metre). x east, y north, meadow z 0, chocolate surface z -8 (river 48 deep).
Hall 4608 x 3840 under a glass roof at z 2048 (WoP's `env/fort-pad512` sunny garden sky through the windows and
roof; the iron glazing bars cast grid shadows via `alphashadow`). Everything except the sky hull and the sealed secret
room is detail: 0 portals, fast compile ~3 min, full bounce light (-bounce 2 -bouncegrid) ~22 min on 2 cores, BSP ~24 MB.

- `build_candy.py` - the map:
  - **Balcony + lobby** (south): grand staircases down both ends, iron railings, colonnade under the balcony (health
    stations), the lobby behind the door (golden ticket, chocolate bars, health, two spawns).
  - **Chocolate falls** (north): the falls (MD3 curtain + turning splash ring) pour off the summit into a deep plunge
    pool - **Splasher on the bottom**. A dry ledge runs behind the falling chocolate into the **rock-candy cave**
    (glowing crystals, Pad shield, Imperius/Boaster ammo) and a square spiral stair inside the cliff climbs to the
    **summit** (Imperius, applause when you get there; a copper outlet pipe feeds the chocolate channel).
  - **Chocolate river**: meanders from the pool to the Tunnel of Wonder in the west wall; wade anywhere, the banks
    slope ~37 deg so you can walk out everywhere (the pool: swim to the river shallows). Candy-cane bridge with a cane
    arch, peppermint stepping stones (Speedy on the river bed), the pink swan boat at its jetty (Boaster).
  - **Suction pipe**: step into the brass intake in the river and the glass pipe slurps you up onto the summit (a
    trigger_push; the glass tube follows the real flight path).
  - **Pump house** (north-west): iron deck on lattice columns, reached by the iron stair-bridge over the river; walk
    into the shed (Pumper); pad to the shed roof (Imperius ammo), pad from the roof to the summit.
  - **Red fly agaric** (centre): walk under it (Bubble G), pad onto the cap (Floater, Betty ammo).
  - **Yellow mushroom house** (south-east): walk in through the door (ammo, toadstool lamp), pad onto the cap
    (Balloony). Hollow MD3 stem with 16-gon wedge collision, door/window openings in both.
  - **Flower tree** (west, rockery mound): pad onto the crepe-paper dome (Betty); a fling pad takes you back down.
  - **Lollipop grove** (south-west): lollipops, two spinning hypno-discs (func_rotating, X axis), twisted candy
    stalks, buttercups, Visionless hidden among them.
  - **East shoulder**: 16u rock stairs up the east wall, pad on to the summit.
  - **SECRET - the Tunnel of Wonder**: wade into the psychedelic river tunnel in the west wall (secret sound) and you
    land in the **Fizzy Lifting Room** (sealed under the world): bubbles lift you to the gallery under the big fan
    (Pad Power, Revival, Imperius ammo); Killer Ducks and Betty ammo on the floor; a WoP teleporter takes you back.
- `candy_models.py` - every custom MD3 (procedural meshes, textures, shaders): mushrooms, mushroom house, flower trees,
  gumball tree, 5 lollipop designs + spinning discs, canes + arch, jawbreakers, urns, buttercups, twisted stalks,
  pump shed, pump, deck frame, deck pipes, suction pipe, waterfall + splash, outlet pipe, swan boat, bridge, pedestal
  lamps, lampposts, roof trusses, wall pipes, rock-candy crystals, fizzy bottles, fan, bonbons, chocolate bars,
  cupcakes, candy flowers, toadstools, boulders, railings.
- `candy_textures.py` - brush textures (original art), `padcandy.shader`, `wop_padcandy.arena`
  (ffa lps team ft syc_ffa syc_tp). Chocolate = `surfaceparm water` + 90% opaque glossy surface.
- `layout.py` - shared constants (river centreline, cliff segments, pool, cave, well, deck, balcony...).
- `routes.txt` - on-foot routes for `aas_check.py --route` (generated by build_candy.py).
- `compile.sh`, `check.sh`, `render.sh`, `botrun.sh`, `package.sh`, `env.sh` (cloud session paths).

      OUT=out GAME=~/Games/wop Q3MAP2=q3map2 BSPC=wopbspc ./check.sh

## NetRadiant Custom (no WoP gamepack)
Unzip `wop_padcandy_source.zip` into a folder the game sees (for example `wop/zz_padcandy.pk3dir/`) and compile:

    q3map2 -game quake3 -fs_basepath "<WoP dir>/" -fs_game wop -meta -custinfoparms maps/wop_padcandy.map
    q3map2 -game quake3 -fs_basepath "<WoP dir>/" -fs_game wop -vis -saveprt maps/wop_padcandy.map
    q3map2 -game quake3 -fs_basepath "<WoP dir>/" -fs_game wop -light -fast -samples 3 -filter -bounce 2 -bouncegrid -custinfoparms maps/wop_padcandy.map
    bspc -forcesidesvisible -optimize -bsp2aas maps/wop_padcandy.bsp

The vis stage just reports "No portals means no vis" (everything is detail) - that is expected.

## Lessons
- Cliff of stacked courses: give every x-chunk its own course heights and a slanted, jittered front - aligned strata
  read as a block wall, staggered ones read as rock. Steep segments need small jitter or the face drifts into rooms
  behind it (the stair well first poked out of a sloped face).
- `carve()` with clamped vertices is not an intersection: clip brushes to a half-space with `clip_half()` (carve
  against a huge box) or water pieces overlap the carved ground by a unit or two.
- Solids standing in the river (boat hull, jetty, stones, stair posts) go into `RIVER_SOLIDS`: carved out of both the
  ground and the water, and then added to the map themselves (first build forgot that - the boat had no deck).
- A model floating on water fails the support audit; make it a slow `func_rotating` model2 (the splash ring).
- A railing on a stair passes the audit when an iron stringer plate sits against the tread ends (wall-mounted).
- Glass as `blendFunc GL_ONE GL_ONE` with a dim `rgbGen const` reads as clear glass in game and in the preview
  (GL_DST_COLOR GL_ONE rendered white in bsp2scene).
- aas_check `--route` only follows walk/jump links: routes starting in 48u-deep water are swim areas and always fail;
  check river exits with the main reachability test instead.
