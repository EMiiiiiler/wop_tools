# Couch Lock Lagoon (wop_padlagoon)

Generator: `wopbuild.py padlagoon gen` (or `OUT=<dir> python3 build_padlagoon.py`). Full pipeline: `wopbuild.py padlagoon all`.

## Design sheet
- Theme + scale: Couch Lock's water park on a corner balcony high up the apartment block (Padman scale ~8u/cm, catalog 1.0, 4096 x 3584 deck, low smoky haze). Facade + sliding door north, brick wing west, glass railings south/east open to Couch Lock's painted night city; festoon lights; the couch is dressed (pillows, blanket, pizza box, remote, controller, popcorn, crisps).
- Pool: 2400 x 1500 sunk into the deck (floor -192, water -16, murky teal fog), 16u stair flights out SW and NE. Coffee-table island (320) standing on the pool floor with a diving board and Couch Lock's table clutter. Pool floats (donut, duck, pizza lilo, ring) circle the island (func_rotating IQM).
- Couch (north, custom MD3): seat 400, back 1000 (walkable), arms 640, 64u crawl space under it.
- Cushion Plunge slide: east arm (640) -> slick flume over the east deck -> pool.
- Bong bubble lift (Couch Lock's bong at 1.5x, SW): walk in, get blasted up onto the wall shelf (1200).
- Sideboard (west, 560) with the telly and two lava lamps; giant lava lamps NW + S; grow-tent cabin (SE) with plants inside and a walkable roof (1016).
- Loot: Imperius wall shelf (bong lift), Betty tent roof, Balloony couch back, Pumper island, Splasher sideboard, Boaster crawl space, Bubble G deck. 3-4 ammo each, some on the pool floor.
- Secret: the drain grate on the pool floor -> the stash box under the world (Pad Power, Revival, Killer Ducks, Pad shield).
- Sounds: water rippling, bong bubbling, telly, snoring, siren + pizza buzzer timers, applause on the shelf.
