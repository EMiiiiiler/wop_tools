# Couch Lock Lagoon (wop_padlagoon)

Generator: `wopbuild.py padlagoon gen` (or `OUT=<dir> python3 build_padlagoon.py`). Full pipeline: `wopbuild.py padlagoon all`.

## Design sheet (reimagined, Nov 2026: the rides are built out of the furniture; the first layout is commit 3a27c05)
- Theme + scale: a water park made from Couch Lock's living room on a corner balcony high up the apartment block (Padman scale ~8u/cm, catalog 1.0, 4096 x 3584 deck, low haze, Couch Lock's painted night city). Story: he fell asleep with the garden hose running.
- Spine (high to low): Bong Tube lift (ride up inside the 1.75x bong, out of the mouthpiece) -> wall shelf 1200 (Imperius) -> Gutter Run (white guttering flume along the south railing, slick) -> the Whirl Pool.
- Couch Tub (north): the couch with its seat cushions pulled off, lined with a tarp and full of water (floor 240, surface 384, steps to the end cushions at 400). Couch Falls spills over the front lip onto a 640-wide flight of steps down into the pool; the 64u crawl cave under the couch is behind the waterfall (Boaster).
- Whirl Pool (centre): round (16-gon) sunk pool, floor -192, water -16, a slow anticlockwise current (four pulsed target_push zones) with the pool floats circling the same way, coffee-table island (320, Pumper, diving board), steps out north (cave) and south. Drain under the island = the secret (stash box).
- Slip'n'Slide: yellow sheet off the east couch arm (640), shoots you under the coffee table.
- Spare cushions: one leaning on the couch = the walk up to the tub (35 deg ramp), two stacked on the south deck (cover, pizza box, CHILL pillow).
- Also: sideboard + telly + lava lamps (Splasher), grow-tent cabin (Betty on the roof), giant lava lamps, loungers, umbrella, hookah, festoon lights.
- Loot: Imperius shelf, Betty tent roof, Balloony couch back, Pumper island, Splasher sideboard, Boaster cave, Bubble G deck; Pad shield on the bottom of the Couch Tub. 3-4 ammo each, some under water.
