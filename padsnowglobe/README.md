# wop_padsnowglobe - Padman's Snow Globe

**Theme / scale.** A winter village sealed inside a giant snow globe standing on a desk. 48u per metre inside the glass
(you are a villager). Outside the glass is the giant's room (desk, mug, books, pencil, desk lamp, window with the night
sky) at roughly 300u per cm: scenery only.

**Playable area.** The snow disc inside the glass: radius 2504 at the snow (sphere centre z 700, radius 2600, top
z 3300). Buildings stay inside r 2150; the tiny train circles at r 2250 just inside the glass.

**Centrepiece.** The skating rink (slick ice, r 560) with the octagonal bandstand on it. Curling-stone pads go up to the bandstand roof.

**Landmarks** (each one can be entered and climbed):

| Zone | Landmark |
|---|---|
| north | Chapel: nave with pews, altar and organ; stone tower with a flat top (Imperius, ski-mound pad) |
| east | Stage windmill: two-storey brick base, the stage roof round the tower, the tower room, flat cap top (Betty, flour-sack pad), turning sails |
| west | Sledging hill: ramp up and a toboggan run down. The snowman's hat holds Balloony (sled pad) |
| north-east | Ice-fishing pond and hut. The hole inside the hut leads down to water with Splasher at the bottom; a teleporter brings you out |
| north-west | Log cabin with an upstairs (Pumper) |
| south-west | Gingerbread bakery (two storeys), pink cottage, toy shop |
| south-east | Winter market: Christmas tree and three chalets |
| south | Padville Halt station: a platform to board the train, plus a ticket office |

**Loot ladder.** From hardest to easiest: Imperius (tower 720), Balloony (hat 924), Betty (cap 962), Pumper (cabin loft), Splasher (fishing hole), Boaster (market), Bubble G (rink).

**Water.** The fishing hole is 136u deep. A teleporter at its bottom brings you out at the hut door.

**Secret.** Walk into the dark arch under the chapel organ. You arrive in the globe's music box under the snow (turning pinned drum, comb and gear), with Pad Power, Revival and Killer Ducks.

**Pads.** All themed: curling stones, ski mound, presents, sled, flour sacks.

**Sightline cover.** Bandstand, Christmas tree, pines, sleighs, crate stacks, street lamps, snowman and hill.

**Sounds.** Wind chimes at the bandstand and in the music box, the mill machinery, an owl.
