# Padman's Christmas: Under the Tree (wop_padxmas3)

Generator: `wopbuild.py padxmas3 gen` (or `OUT=<dir> python3 build_padxmas3.py`). Full pipeline: `wopbuild.py padxmas3 all`.

## Design sheet
- Theme + scale: padxmas re-imagined from UNDER the tree, inside a cosy living room at true scale (~16u per cm:
  Padman 3.5 cm tall, the 2.4 m ceiling at 3840u, the tree floor to almost ceiling with a gold star). Room
  -3328..4368 x -2304..2304: green damask wallpaper over walnut wainscot + dado rail, dark skirting, crown moulding,
  a three-pane window high on the south wall onto the snowy night (env/frost-bite512), wine carpet, felt tree skirt.
- Centrepiece: green plastic tree stand (832u, rim 320) full of water, four chrome clamp screws as bridges to the trunk,
  sloped feet E/W as ramps, 16u steps out of the water; the canopy (4 whorls of needle-card boughs, baubles, icicles,
  candy canes, stars, bells, gingerbread men, fairy-light swags) and one walkable lowest branch (1104 -> 984).
- Fireplace (W wall): brick chimney breast, lit log fire, tiled hearth (16u), oak surround, mantel at 1216 (288 deep)
  with clock, candlesticks, cards, garland, stockings, a winter painting above. The flames teleport you onto the mantel,
  bellows pads at both ends, a gold-bow fling onto the tube top. Footstool (throw-pillow pad), log basket, fire tools,
  woodpile, a sleeping cat.
- Sofa corner (E wall): ochre velvet three-seater (seat 720, back 1360) and a tartan wingback, 224u of crawl space under
  both (front valance with a gap); whoopee cushion -> seat, throw pillow -> sofa back, ball of yarn -> armchair seat;
  the unwrapping mess in front (torn paper, crumpled balls, opened boxes, ribbon curls, slippers), a standing lamp.
- Zones round the tree: present tower NE (walk-in, slinky/bow pads), gingerbread house SW (door, windows, iced roof,
  chocolate 16u stairs + gumdrop pad) + candy village, broken bauble SE (walk inside), wrapping-paper tube NW (tunnel,
  paper-sheet ramp, gumdrop pad), toy train + present tunnel S, milk & cookies for Santa NW, nutcrackers, snow globe,
  rocking horse, teddy, jack-in-the-box, turning Christmas pyramid (func_rotating), sleigh, drum, mouse hole.
- Loot ladder: Imperius mantel | Betty branch tip | Balloony sofa back | Pumper inside the tube | Splasher bottom of
  the stand's water | Boaster inside the bauble | Bubble G cookie plate. 4-6 ammo spots each; BamBam on the top present.
- Secret: drain grate on the bowl floor (under water) -> inside a gold present (Pad Power, Revival, Killer Ducks).
- Sounds: fire crackling, water rippling, electric sizzle (socket), clock ticking, wind chimes, kitten, snoring (secret).
