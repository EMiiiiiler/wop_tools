# Padman's Tabletop Quest (wop_padtabletop)

Generator: `wopbuild.py padtabletop gen` (or `OUT=<dir> python3 build_padtabletop.py`). Full pipeline: `wopbuild.py padtabletop all`.

## Design sheet
- Theme + scale: a Dungeons & Dragons gaming table (from the user's photo). Padman is a miniature: 1 mat square = 128u,
  dungeon walls 2" (256u), rail top 576. Catalog trees ~0.8-1.0. Sky box = the games room (painted, soft focus).
- Playable area: mat 4608 x 3840 inside a walkable wooden rail (384 wide, z 576).
- Centrepiece: the dragon (custom MD3) on the upper plateau (z 512) with its gold hoard.
- Zones: forest strip (ruined cottage with a deck, pond, ruined wall), 13-room modular dungeon (campfire hall, tavern,
  storage, barracks, forge, armory, shrine, library, crypt, well room, stair hall), east clearing, lower terrace z 256
  (crenellated rampart, tunnel crypt <-> clearing), upper plateau z 512, rail (dice, mug, rulebooks, Padmaster screen,
  dice tower).
- Climbs: grand stairs (dungeon -> terrace), west rubble ramp, rock stairs terrace -> plateau, stairs terrace -> rail
  (west) and plateau -> rail (east); themed pads (pressure plates, rune circles, cork coasters).
- Loot: Imperius dice-tower top, Betty dragon hoard, Balloony rulebook stack, Pumper tunnel, Splasher pond bottom,
  Boaster campfire hall, Bubble G cottage.
- Water: forest pond (104 deep) with 16u rock steps out at the west end.
- Secret: hop into the well (NW room) -> "Behind the Padmaster's Screen" (Pad Power, Revival, Killer Ducks, shield).
- Sounds: campfire, tunnel drips, pond, owl, dragon breath, applause on the dice tower.
